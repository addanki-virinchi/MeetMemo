Getting started

  1. Sign in. Click the MeetMemo icon in the Chrome toolbar and sign in with Google or with your email and password.
  2. Join a Google Meet call. The MeetMemo panel opens on the right side of the meeting.
  3. Let people talk. MeetMemo uses Google Meet's live captions. The transcript appears in the panel and is saved to your account every few seconds.
  4. Read it later. Open Meetings (or the Home button in the panel) to find every saved meeting.

You don't need to keep the panel visible. Minimise it and MeetMemo keeps saving in the background while the meeting tab is open.

Languages

MeetMemo saves exactly what Google Meet captions, in the language being spoken. It does not translate while recording: a meeting held in Spanish is saved in Spanish.

Transcribing a meeting in another language

During the call, select Change next to “Transcribing: …” at the top of the panel and choose the language people are speaking. This sets Google Meet's caption language for you. Choosing the wrong language produces poor captions, so match the spoken language. If the language changes partway through the meeting, change it again.

Reading a transcript in your language

After the meeting, open the transcript and use Read in to translate it for reading. This works in Chrome 138 or later and runs entirely on your device. Set a default in Settings → Reading language. The saved transcript always stays in its original language.

Your transcripts

  • Find a meeting: Meetings lists everything newest first, grouped by day. Search by meeting code, title or the words in the preview.
  • Copy, download or print: open a transcript and use the buttons above the text. “Print or save as PDF” produces a clean document for sharing.
  • Delete: use the bin icon next to a meeting. Deletion is permanent and removes every line of that transcript from the server.

Troubleshooting

The panel asks me to sign in, but I'm already signed in

Refresh the Google Meet tab. If the prompt stays, click the MeetMemo toolbar icon to confirm you are signed in. AI features show a separate “coming soon” message, which is expected.

Nothing is being saved

Check, in order:

  1. You are signed in (toolbar icon → your email is shown).
  2. The panel is not paused.
  3. Someone is speaking, and text appears in the panel. MeetMemo saves only what Meet captions.
  4. The toolbar popup's Live sync section says “Saving…” or “Saved”. If it shows an error, it retries automatically.
Meetings take a long time to load the first time

The MeetMemo server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while and takes up to a minute to wake up. Lines you speak meanwhile are kept on your computer and uploaded as soon as the server answers, so nothing is lost.

The transcript has wrong or strange words

The caption language probably doesn't match the spoken language. Use Change in the panel to pick the right one. Background noise and people speaking over each other also reduce caption quality.

The panel doesn't appear in my meeting

MeetMemo works on Google Meet meeting pages (meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij). Refresh the meeting tab. If you just installed or updated the extension, reload it at chrome://extensions first.

“Read in” is missing on the transcript page

On-device translation needs Chrome 138 or later on desktop. On other browsers the transcript is shown in its original language.
