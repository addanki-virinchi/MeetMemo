Privacy

How MeetMemo handles your meetings

What is saved, where it goes, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Text only

MeetMemo saves caption text. It never records audio, video or your screen.

Private to you

Every transcript belongs to your account. No one else can open it, and there are no public links.

You're in control

Pause any time. Delete any meeting permanently from Meetings.

No tracking

No analytics or advertising trackers. Your transcripts are not sold or shared.

What MeetMemo saves

While you are signed in and a Google Meet call is open with MeetMemo running, MeetMemo saves:

  • Transcript lines: the text of Google Meet's live captions, the speaker name as Google Meet shows it, and the time each line was spoken.
  • Meeting details: the meeting code (for example abc-defg-hij), the platform, and when the meeting started and was last saved.
  • Your account: your name, email address and profile picture link from sign-in, plus when you last signed in.

What MeetMemo does not save

  • Audio or video of the meeting, or anything on screen. Screenshots are not available.
  • Meeting chat messages.
  • Anything said while the panel is paused.
  • Your password. Passwords go directly to Google's Firebase Authentication service, and MeetMemo never stores them.

The captions themselves are produced by Google Meet, under Google's terms, whether or not MeetMemo is installed. MeetMemo reads the captions Meet already shows you.

Where your data is stored

  • MeetMemo server: transcripts are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the MeetMemo service, which is hosted on Render.
  • Database: the server stores them in Google Cloud Firestore, which encrypts data at rest. The database refuses every direct connection; only the MeetMemo server can read or write it.
  • On your computer: lines waiting to be uploaded are kept briefly in your browser's extension storage, so nothing is lost if the connection drops. Lines are removed from your computer once saved. Lines waiting for a different account to sign in are kept for up to 7 days. Your sign-in session is also stored in the browser.

Who can see your transcripts

Only you. Every request is checked against your signed-in account: another user who guessed a meeting ID would get “not found”. MeetMemo has no public share links. When you choose Share, the transcript opens for you, and you decide what to copy, download or send.

Server logs record technical errors, never transcript text.

Translation and AI

Read in translation runs on your own device with Chrome's built-in translator. Your text is not sent to MeetMemo or anyone else to be translated.

Ask MeetMemo AI

Nothing is sent for AI until you ask a question. When you do:

  • What is sent: your question, earlier questions and answers in the same conversation, and that one meeting's saved transcript (speaker names, times and text). Nothing from your other meetings.
  • Where it goes: the MeetMemo server sends it to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the answer. Your browser never contacts Anthropic, and the API key stays on the MeetMemo server.
  • What is saved: your questions and the answers are stored with the meeting in your MeetMemo account, so the conversation is there next time. Only you can see them.
  • Deleting: deleting a meeting also deletes all of its AI conversations.

Server logs record which meeting was asked about, timing and token counts, never your question, the transcript or the answer.

MeetMemo AI auto-answer (live meetings)

Auto-answer is off for every meeting until you turn it on in that meeting's live view. While it is on:

  • What is checked on your device: live captions are checked in your browser for things that look like questions. Greetings and audio checks ("Can you hear me?") are dropped there and never sent.
  • What is sent: for each likely question, the question and a short excerpt of the last few transcript lines (up to 12, with speaker names and times) go to the MeetMemo server, which sends them to Anthropic's Claude API. Claude first decides whether the question is worth answering; small talk gets no answer.
  • What is saved: answered questions, the answer and the lines it cites are stored with the meeting in your account. The transcript excerpt is not stored with the answer. Only you can see the answers.
  • Turning it off: switching Auto-answer off, signing out or switching accounts stops new answers immediately. Deleting the meeting deletes its answers.

Analytics and tracking

MeetMemo does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The meeting panel contains older analytics code from the product it was built on; this version blocks that code from sending anything.

Telling participants

Let people know when you transcribe a meeting; in many places the law requires it. In the panel, open the Privacy (shield) menu and select Notify in chat to post a short notice in the meeting chat.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • Transcripts are kept until you delete them.
  • Delete a meeting: in Meetings, select the bin icon. The meeting and every line of its transcript are removed from the server immediately and cannot be recovered.
  • Sign out: removes your session from this browser. Your saved transcripts stay in your account.
  • Remove everything: delete your meetings, then ask your MeetMemo administrator to delete your account.
