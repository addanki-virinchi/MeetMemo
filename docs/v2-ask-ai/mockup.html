Layouts considered

All three keep the transcript reachable. The side panel is the only one where the transcript and the conversation are both visible at the same time, which matters because every answer links back to transcript lines.

A · Panel above transcript

Your initial idea

  • Very simple to build, works at any width
  • Each answer pushes the transcript down the page
  • Follow-ups make the panel grow or scroll inside a box
  • A time chip scrolls the answer out of view

B · Docked side panel Recommended

Shown live above

  • Transcript and answers side by side; time chips jump without losing the answer
  • Chat layout handles follow-ups naturally
  • Room for later AI features (summary, action items) as panel tabs
  • Needs a tab layout below ~1080px (included)

C · Slide-over drawer

Common pattern

  • Leaves the reader layout untouched when closed
  • Covers the transcript lines the answer cites
  • Feels like a separate tool, not part of the meeting

How a question flows

The same steps in the order you described them, as the user sees them.

1

Open a meeting

The panel opens beside the transcript with suggested questions that use the meeting's own speaker names.

2

Ask

Type or pick a suggestion. The question appears at once and the send button becomes Stop.

3

Loading

"Reading the transcript…" with placeholder lines, then the answer streams in word by word.

4

Answer

Formatted text with time chips. A chip scrolls to and highlights that line in the transcript.

5

Follow up

Ask again in the same box. Earlier turns go along as context, so "who owns that?" works.

6

Come back later

The conversation is saved with the meeting and reappears next time. Deleting the meeting deletes it.
