Meeting platforms

MeetMemo works the same way in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams when the meeting runs in this browser: the panel, saved transcripts, Claude and the assist buttons. Upwork client calls held on Zoom or Google Meet are saved as “Upwork client call”.

Zoom only shows captions to MeetMemo in its web client. When this is on, Zoom meeting links (including the ones Upwork posts in Messages) open in the browser instead of the Zoom app.

Transcription language

MeetMemo saves the meeting's live captions (Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams) in the language that is being spoken. It does not translate while you record.

Language of the meeting
Set it during the call: in the MeetMemo panel, select Change next to “Transcribing: …”. This switches the meeting's caption language, so pick the language people are actually speaking. In Zoom and Teams the caption language can also be set from the meeting's own captions menu.

Reading and translation

Read a saved transcript in another language after the meeting. Translation runs on this device with Chrome's built-in translator; your saved transcript is never changed.
