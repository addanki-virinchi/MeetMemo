Transcription language

MeetMemo saves Google Meet's live captions in the language that is being spoken. It does not translate while you record.

Language of the meeting
Set it during the call: in the MeetMemo panel, select Change next to “Transcribing: …”. This switches Google Meet's caption language, so pick the language people are actually speaking. Google Meet supports many languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese and Korean.
